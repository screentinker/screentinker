Getting started with ScreenTinker

Set up your first digital signage screen and start showing content. This guide walks you through account creation, adding a screen, uploading content, and verifying it's working.

This guide covers the entire setup process for new users. It starts with signing in or creating an account, then walks you through adding your first screen using the onboarding wizard, uploading content during setup, and checking that everything is working properly. We'll also explain the difference between Simple and Advanced dashboard views.

Sign in or create an account

When you go to the ScreenTinker website, you'll land on the login page. If you're the first user, you'll be asked to create an admin account. For hosted instances, new accounts get a 14-day free Pro trial. Sign in or create your account using your email and a password of 8 or more characters.

Password requirement

The form accepts passwords with at least 6 characters, but the server requires 8 or more. If you try to create an account with a 6- or 7-character password, it will pass the form validation but be rejected by the server.

Add your first screen

After signing in, you can add your first screen. New accounts start in Simple view, which makes adding a screen easy.

  1. In Simple view, click the Add a screen tile (or go to Screens → Add screen in Advanced view).
  2. The onboarding wizard will guide you through connecting your display:
    • Open the ScreenTinker player app on your TV or device
    • Enter the pairing code shown on the display in the dashboard
    • Name your screen (e.g., "Lobby TV")
  3. If you're using a web browser to try the player, you can use the web player link instead of a pairing code.

After connecting, the system will automatically publish a playlist with sample content for you. If you're using the Advanced dashboard, you'll see a "Getting started" checklist that guides you through each step.

Screen names

If you don't enter a name, the screen will be called "Display N". You can change this later on the screen's detail page.

Add content to your screen

During the setup process, you can upload your own photos or videos. The system creates a new playlist for your screen and automatically publishes it for you.

  1. After connecting your screen, you'll be prompted to add content.
  2. You can use sample images provided by ScreenTinker, create a sign with the built-in editor, or upload your own files.
  3. Supported formats include MP4, WebM, JPEG, PNG, GIF, MP3, and WAV.
  4. Multiple files are allowed — they'll be added to the screen's playlist in the order you select them.
Content is published automatically

During the onboarding process, your content is automatically published for you. In the full dashboard, you'd need to press Publish after uploading content.

Check it's working

Once your screen is connected and showing content, you can verify it's working properly:

  1. In the dashboard, look for your screen in the list of displays.
  2. The screen should show a status of Online or Playing.
  3. Click on the screen to see its detail page and check that it's showing the correct content.
  4. On the screen's detail page, you can take screenshots or view what's currently playing.

On the screen detail page, under the "Now Playing" tab, you'll find a screenshot button and a preview button. The screenshot shows what's currently displayed on the screen.

Updates are quick

Screens update within a few seconds of making changes to content or playlists.

Simple vs Advanced view

New accounts start in Simple view, which is designed for ease of use:

  • Simple view (default): Shows a dashboard with task tiles for common actions like "Add a screen", "Change what a screen shows", and "Upload photos". It's easier for new users.
  • Advanced view: Shows the full dashboard with sidebar menus, including Screens, Schedules, Playlists, and Settings. Use this when you need to perform advanced tasks like creating schedules or managing user permissions.

To switch between views, click the Simple / Advanced buttons at the top of the menu.

Next steps

After setting up your first screen, you can:

  • Create additional screens by repeating the setup process
  • Add more content to your screen's playlist
  • Create schedules to show different playlists at different times
  • Organize screens into groups for easier management
  • Set up custom layouts and transitions for your content

You can also explore the full dashboard in Advanced view to see all the features available in ScreenTinker.

FAQ

How do I add more screens?

After setting up your first screen, you can add additional screens by going to the Screens section in the dashboard and clicking "Add screen". Follow the same steps as for your first screen.

What if my screen doesn't show up?

Check that your screen is online and connected to the internet. Make sure you've entered the correct pairing code from your display. If the pairing code has expired (after 15 minutes), restart your display to get a new code.

Can I use the same content on multiple screens?

Yes! You can create playlists with the same content and assign them to multiple screens. You can also organize screens into groups and assign playlists to entire groups at once.
