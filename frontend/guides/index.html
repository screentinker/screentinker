Digital signage guides

How to put ScreenTinker on the screens you already have, how to run your own server, and how to build what plays on them. Pick your device below; each guide goes from unboxing to a screen showing your content.

Set up a screen

  • Raspberry Pi. A browser kiosk or the native player on a Pi 3, 4 or 5.
  • Android TV and Fire TV. The free player app on any Android TV box, Fire TV Stick or tablet, with kiosk mode.
  • Fire TV Stick 4K Select and HD (Vega OS). The newer sticks that run Vega OS, not Android, and the player for them.
  • Samsung TVs and signage displays. Tizen panels through the built-in URL Launcher or the player app.
  • LG webOS Signage. The player app on LG webOS Signage panels, installed from your own server.
  • Roku. What the Roku player plays, and what a Roku cannot do as a sign.
  • BrightSign. One autorun.zip from your server, dual output, wall sync and panel power.
  • Windows. Any Windows 10 or 11 PC as a player that stays up unattended.
  • Mac, iPad and iPhone. The native macOS player, and the app for iPad and iPhone.
  • ChromeOS. Chromeboxes and Chromebooks, managed through Google Admin or on their own.
  • LED video walls. Driving a large LED wall at its native resolution.
  • E-paper and ESP32 signs. Low-power signs with no browser on the device at all.
  • Apple TV. Why Apple TV cannot run a sign, and what to use instead.

Not sure which device to buy? See Certified Hardware. Already have a server? Its download page lists every player it can hand out.
