Report hardware that works

Running ScreenTinker on something that is not on the list? Tell us and we will publish it so the next person choosing hardware has one more data point.

This is not certification. Reports are published under Community reported, which means a user says it works. ScreenTinker will not have tested it and keeps no unit of it, so it is not Certified Hardware and carries no support commitment.

Certification is a separate thing: ScreenTinker tests the device, keeps one in the lab, and support obligations attach to it. If you need a specific model certified, contact support.
