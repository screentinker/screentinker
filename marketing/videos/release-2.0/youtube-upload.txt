ScreenTinker 2.0 — Design Slides, Fire Triggers, Federate Servers

ScreenTinker 2.0: A Real Slide Editor for Open-Source Digital Signage


---------------------------- DESCRIPTION -------------------------------

ScreenTinker 2.0 is out — the biggest release the project has had. Most of
it is one thing: you can finally design a slide, properly, inside the
product.

▶ What's new

SLIDES — a real authoring surface. Headlines, body text, big numbers,
photos, rules and panels, plus clock, date, countdown and QR elements. QR
codes are drawn server-side, so a code needs no network at the panel and no
third-party image service.

The part that matters: a slide is two separate things. The template is the
view — geometry, style, motion, a slot name per element. The fields are the
record — just the words. They meet at render time and nowhere else, so
editing a headline three months later writes one string and leaves the
layout untouched. Fifteen signage products were surveyed before this was
designed, and not one of them makes the changeable text part of the
template.

Also in Slides: picture and video backgrounds with a scrim, bundled fonts
served with the slide so a deck renders the same on Android, Tizen,
BrightSign or a browser, per-element motion that warns you when an
animation outlives its slide's dwell, portrait authoring, and generated
then layered slides with real transparency.

TRIGGERS — an external system (a Crestron or Extron panel, a PLC, a button)
can put a playlist over whatever a screen is showing. Fired over HTTP or
UDP in four wire formats, and resolved on the screen itself, so an alarm
still works with the WAN down.

NODE MESH — servers can federate. A hub can see a customer's screens, send
content, request a reboot and read diagnostics, each under its own grant,
with the customer deciding what it accepts. Deliberately conservative for a
first release: opt-in, capped at two tiers.

RUNNING THE SERVER ON THE PLAYER — a BrightSign can now run ScreenTinker
itself, with the player in the widget beside it.

PROOF OF PLAY THAT SURVIVES AN OUTAGE — players queue what they played
offline and flush it on reconnect, de-duplicated by a player-minted id so a
re-flush cannot double-count.

PLUS — a second workspace per account, per-organisation SSO/OIDC with
DNS-verified domains, HTML bundles (.wgt/.zip) as a playlist item, bulk
selection and group actions, playlist inheritance that forks instead of
overwriting, Japanese localisation, and an SBOM published with every
release.

⚠️ The Designer is marked deprecating. Widgets made with it still play and
are still editable there — it is not removed — but new work belongs in
Slides.

▶ Upgrading from 1.9.x
Nothing to do by hand. Migrations run on first boot. Content, playlists,
schedules and pairings all keep their meaning, and the mesh stays off until
you turn it on.
