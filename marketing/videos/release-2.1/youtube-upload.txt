---------------------------- DESCRIPTION -------------------------------

Everything ScreenTinker picked up since 2.0: live video of what a screen is
actually showing, a voice/PA channel to your screens, live data bound straight
into slides, meeting-room signs that speak every language, and a plugin system.
ScreenTinker is open source: use the hosted option and we run it for you, or host
it yourself. (Every scene in this video is a real ScreenTinker slide.)

In this video:
- Live view: watch a display's real output, sub-second, over WebRTC.
- Talk: a voice intercom and public-address channel to your screens.
- Data sources: bind a calendar or any JSON feed into a slide or widget.
- Meeting-room signs: Busy / Available in every dashboard language (defaults to English).
- Plugins: add widget types, data connectors and hooks without forking. Off by default, and a plugin never runs until an admin approves that exact copy.
- PDF to playlist: every page becomes a full-screen slide, in the browser.
- Approval and version history for teams.
- More screens: LG webOS, e-paper and microcontrollers, plus a certified-hardware list.
- Player polish: custom transition shaders, no black flash between clips, portrait panels that fill the screen.

Source: https://github.com/screentinker/screentinker
Site:   https://screentinker.com
A star on the repository genuinely helps.

And the fun part: this whole video was built and tested in ScreenTinker. Every slide is a real ScreenTinker slide, playing on a real ScreenTinker screen.
