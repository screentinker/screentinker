Free Digital Signage on a Samsung TV (No App, No Box, No Monthly Fees)


---------------------------- DESCRIPTION -------------------------------

Turn a Samsung TV into a fully managed digital signage display — with no
extra box, no app to install, and no monthly fees. ScreenTinker is open
source and self-hostable, so there are no per-screen fees and your content
stays on your own server.

The trick: the player is just a web page. Point your Samsung display's URL
Launcher (or its built-in browser) at your server's /player address, save it
as the boot source, and the screen opens straight into a live sign — pair it
with a 6-digit code and push content from the dashboard.

Works on Samsung signage / business displays (SSSP) with a URL Launcher, and
Samsung TVs with a built-in web browser. No MagicINFO, no license.
