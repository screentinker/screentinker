Stop Paying Per Screen — Free Open-Source Alternative to Yodeck & OptiSigns


---------------------------- DESCRIPTION -------------------------------

Yodeck, ScreenCloud, and OptiSigns are all solid digital signage products —
and they all charge you per screen, every month. ScreenTinker is a free,
open-source, self-hostable alternative that does the same job: playlists,
scheduling, multi-zone layouts, synchronized video walls, widgets, and
proof-of-play — on hardware you already own.

Self hosting ScreenTinker is completely free, on any number of screens — you
only pay for the server. A hosted plan exists too, at a flat $99/mo for 15
devices, if you would rather not run one.

This is an honest, fair comparison. I cover where the paid tools genuinely
win (Yodeck's pre-configured Pi players, ScreenCloud's Slack/Power BI apps,
OptiSigns' template library), and where the open-source option pulls ahead:
per-screen cost at scale, data sovereignty, and no vendor lock-in.
