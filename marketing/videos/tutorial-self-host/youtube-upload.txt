Self-Host Free Digital Signage on a $5 VPS (Docker, 5 Minutes)

Self-Hosted Digital Signage with Docker — Open Source, No Monthly Fees


---------------------------- DESCRIPTION -------------------------------

Run your own free, open-source digital signage server — no per-screen fees,
no vendor lock-in, and your content and data never leave your infrastructure.
With Docker, the whole platform (dashboard + web player + API) is up in about
five minutes on a $5 VPS.

The whole install is: grab the compose file, `docker compose up -d`, register
the first admin, and pair a display. Optionally front it with Caddy for
automatic HTTPS — or run it plain on your LAN, fully air-gapped.

You get the entire platform, nothing paywalled: playlists, scheduling,
multi-zone layouts, video walls, widgets, proof-of-play analytics, multiple
workspaces, and a scoped REST API.
